What is this?

This is a grocery list app!

Goals:
- Cross-platform, specifically Android, iOS, and the web. Maybe a Windows app in the future, but not right now
- No infrastructure in the middle: peer-to-peer syncing to keep things nice and simple
- Esay to use: adding and removing items from the list should be as easy as possible.

Other features:
- Group items by store
- Share lists with groups of people
- Distinction between removed, bought, and consumed, and expired
